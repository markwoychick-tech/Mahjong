Set Up the Game

Choose single-player practice or team play. Round 1 uses beginner clues, Double Jeopardy adds strategy, and Final Jeopardy uses a wagered end-game scenario.

How to Play

Answer format: players can answer normally or use Jeopardy style if you prefer. After each response, reveal the answer and mark the team correct or incorrect.

Mahjong focus: this version assumes American Mahjong, including the National Mah Jongg League-style card, Charleston, jokers, exposures, and table strategy.
